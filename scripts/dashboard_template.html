Implementation

The pipeline, end to end

Eight modules, one direction of data flow. Every stage is independently testable and driven by config/default.yaml.

1

Collect

URLhaus · ThreatFox · Feodo (OTX & MISP with keys). DoH fallback for broken resolvers.

feeds/ · collector.py
2

Store

WAL-mode SQLite, dedup by value, TTL expiry, poll snapshots as CSV.

store.py → data/ioc_store.db
3

Replay

Flows replayed in time order against a feed polled every Δ — visibility from the next poll boundary.

enrich.py · poll_grid()
4

Enrich

6 CTI features per flow: IoC match, age score, confidence, source count, reputation.

enrich.py · CTI_FEATURES
5

Model

64/16/20 split, RF with balanced leaves, θ tuned on validation for 96 % recall.

model.py
6

Alert

Test rows above θ become alerts with score, label and ground-truth category.

run_e4 · explain_alerts()
7

Explain

TreeSHAP per alert → top-k reasons with human-readable text and feature values.

explain.py · reason_codes()
8

Map

Reasons mapped to ATT&CK technique + tactic; stability audited across 5 folds.

attack_map.py
